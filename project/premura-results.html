How These Results Happen

The same model produced
every number above

None of these clients bought a magic list of leads. Each one got the same thing: a dedicated call center Premura built and runs for them. Here is what that actually looks like.

  1. Dedicated agents on your territory only

    Each client gets agents who call that client's own zip codes and no one else's. The appointments are exclusive to them, which is why the calendar fills instead of competing operators chasing the same homeowners.

    Recruited, trained, and managed by Premura, coached daily.

  2. Seven to eight hours of dialing a day

    Setters dial the full day on in-house data that is scraped and refreshed weekly. The math is simple and consistent: around one qualified appointment per agent per day, booked straight into the calendar.

    Scripts and QA that the client approves, so the pitch is theirs.

  3. Closers who work the appointments

    Every result on this page came from a client who could close and service the volume we sent. We book qualified appointments, and the client's team turns them into deals and installs.

    A live dashboard shows the numbers as they happen, not in a monthly recap.

This is why we are careful with the word results. The agents create opportunities. The client's ability to close is what turns 22 booked appointments into 22 deals. The two work together.

The Honest Caveat

Individual results,
not a promise to you

We will not tell you that you will close 17 deals in 30 days, because we do not know your market, your closers, or your offer. Scott, Leicer, Jorge, Caesar, Rafa, and Mitchell reported their own numbers in their own situations. Yours could be higher or lower.

What we can promise is the process behind the numbers: dedicated agents dialing your territory all day, qualified appointments on your calendar, daily management, and a dashboard so you see exactly what is happening. If you want an honest read on what is realistic for your company specifically, a strategy call is the place to get it.

Straight Answers

Premura results,
the questions people ask

What results have Premura clients actually reported?

On camera, named clients report figures like Scott Kelly closing 17 deals in his first 30 days, Leicer closing 22 deals in 30 days, Jorge booking 20 or more deals a month, Caesar completing 8 to 10 installs a month, Rafa closing 12 or more deals a month, and Mitchell Rossey booking 50 to 60 appointments a month. Michael Velardi is one of several solar operators who share their experience as well. You can watch these testimonials on our reviews page.

Are these Premura results typical or guaranteed?

No. These are individual results that specific clients stated in their own videos, not averages or promises. Outcomes depend on your closers, your territory, your offer, and how well you work the appointments we book. We do not guarantee a number, and we are careful never to present one person's result as what everyone should expect.

How does Premura produce these results?

Each client gets dedicated agents who call only that client's territory. Setters dial seven to eight hours a day and produce around one qualified appointment per agent per day, booked straight into the client's calendar. The agents are recruited, trained, and managed by Premura, with scripts and QA the client approves and a live dashboard to watch the numbers.

Can I verify these testimonials are real?

Yes. The people above appear by name on video on our reviews page, where you can watch them describe their own numbers. We only publish results a real client stated on camera, and we link out so you can judge them for yourself rather than taking our word for it.

Will I get the same results as Scott or Leicer?

Maybe more, maybe less. Their numbers reflect their markets, their closers, and their effort, not a fixed outcome we can promise you. The honest way to find out what is realistic for your company is a strategy call where we look at your territory and capacity together.
