The Honest Answer

Why we do not sell you
a money-back guarantee

A guarantee sounds reassuring on a sales page. In cold calling, it is usually a red flag.

Nobody can guarantee that a homeowner will say yes. Any company that promises you a fixed outcome is either not being straight or is hiding the escape hatch in the fine print. We think the more honest thing is to remove risk structurally, through how the engagement is set up and run, rather than to paper over it with a slogan. So instead of a guarantee, we point to five concrete things that protect you, and we are equally clear about the one part of the equation we do not control.

What Actually Lowers Your Risk

Five things that de-risk it,
none of them a slogan

These are built into how the service works, not bolted on as a marketing promise.

  1. No long-term lock-in to start

    You are not signing away years before you know it works. Starting without a multi-year commitment is the single biggest thing that lowers your downside, because if it is not producing you are not trapped.

  2. You approve the scripts and QA

    Nothing gets said to your prospects that you have not signed off on. You approve the scripts and the quality-assurance standards, so the campaign represents your brand the way you want it to from the first dial.

  3. A live dashboard, not a monthly report

    You watch dials, contacts, and appointments on a live dashboard. If something is off, you see it in days, not at the end of a billing cycle, and so do we.

  4. Daily coaching of your agents

    Your dedicated agents are coached every day. Cold calling drifts without daily management, so we listen, correct, and retrain rather than letting a slow week become a slow month.

  5. We find and fix the weak spots

    When results lag, there is almost always a specific, fixable cause: a script line that stalls, a list that needs refreshing, a rebuttal that is not landing. We hunt for it and fix it instead of shrugging.

Being Straight

The part that depends
on you, not on us

A full calendar is only half the job. This is the honest boundary of what a call center can do.

What we control

Trained, dedicated agents dialing your territory 7 to 8 hours a day, booking qualified appointments straight into your calendar at a realistic pace of about one per agent per day.

What you control

Your closer. Booked appointments have to be called quickly and worked well. A weak or slow closing process will underperform even a strong flow of appointments.

Where results come from

The best outcomes on the reviews page happened where a full calendar met a good closer. We fill the calendar honestly. You close the deals.

We would rather tell you this up front than let you discover it later. If you do not have someone to run the appointments, the smartest move is to fix that first, because no appointment engine rescues a closing process that is not there.

What If It Doesn't Work

If the appointments
are not coming

A slow start is usually a diagnosis, not a dead end. Here is how we treat it.

  • We look at the live numbers first, because the dashboard shows exactly where the drop-off is happening.
  • We check the script and the rebuttals, since a single weak line early in the call can sink a whole campaign.
  • We refresh the calling data, which our in-house software updates weekly, so agents are not burning a stale list.
  • We coach or replace agents who are not performing, rather than leaving a seat underdelivering.
  • We confirm your side of it, that booked appointments are being called quickly, because speed to lead changes everything.

And because there is no long-term lock-in to begin with, you are never forced to keep paying into something that genuinely is not fitting your business. That freedom to walk is a more honest protection than a guarantee with an asterisk.

Common questions

Does Premura offer a guarantee?

We do not advertise a money-back guarantee, and we would be careful of any cold-calling company that promises one, because no honest agency can guarantee a homeowner's decision. What we do offer is structural risk reduction: no long-term lock-in to start, scripts and quality assurance you approve, a live dashboard, daily coaching of your agents, and active work to find and fix weak spots.

What if Premura doesn't work for my company?

Because there is no long-term lock-in to start, you are not trapped for years if it is not producing. The more common cause of a slow start is a fixable one, such as a script that needs tuning or appointments that are not being worked fast enough. We monitor the campaign daily, coach the agents, and adjust rather than letting a weak spot sit.

How does Premura reduce my risk without a guarantee?

Five ways. There is no long-term lock-in to begin, so you are not committing for years up front. You approve the scripts and the quality-assurance standards before agents dial. You watch results on a live dashboard instead of waiting for a monthly report. Agents get daily coaching. And we actively look for and fix weak spots in the campaign.

Do the results depend on me too?

Yes, and we will say so plainly. Premura fills your calendar with qualified appointments, but your closer still has to run them. If booked appointments are not called quickly or the closer is weak, even a strong appointment flow will underperform. The best results come when a good closing process meets a full calendar.

Why not just promise a set number of appointments?

We manage to a realistic pace, roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day with setters dialing 7 to 8 hours a day, and we are transparent about it through the dashboard. Wrapping that in a hard guarantee would either be dishonest or padded with fine print. We would rather show you real, daily numbers than sell you a promise.
