Why In-House Cold Calling Stalls

Cold calling is a full-time job
you never signed up to manage

Most owners do not have a lead problem. They have a dialing problem. The phone works, but nobody has 8 hours a day to sit on it, and hiring someone to do it opens a second business you did not want to run.

  1. Reps wash out faster than you can hire them

    Cold calling has brutal turnover. You hire two setters, one quits inside a month, and the other slows down the week the calls get hard. Now you are recruiting again instead of closing deals.

    Our version: 160+ trained callers already on the floor, replaced by us until yours perform.

  2. Nobody dials a full day

    An in-house rep between other tasks makes 40 calls and calls it a day. Booking appointments is a volume game, and volume only happens when someone is on the phone for hours, not minutes.

    Our version: dedicated setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day, every day, against fresh lists.

  3. The data goes stale

    Buying a list once and burning through it means your callers are chasing disconnected numbers by week two. Good cold calling runs on data that gets refreshed, filtered to your area, and cleaned.

    Our version: in-house scraping software refreshed weekly, filtered to the zip codes you pick.

  4. No one is coaching the pitch

    A script only gets good by hearing it fail on live calls and fixing it. Without daily listening and coaching, your rep repeats the same losing line for weeks and blames the market.

    Our version: daily coaching and QA on a script you approve, tuned campaign by campaign.

How It Works

From your zip codes
to a booked calendar

A simple path from the first call with us to appointments landing on your closers' calendars. See the full breakdown on how it works.

  1. You pick the territory

    You tell us the zip codes you want worked and the offer you sell. We load fresh data for that area and build a call list your dedicated setters work through.

  2. We build and you approve the script

    We write the opener, the qualification questions, and the close to a calendar slot, then hand it to you for approval. Nothing goes live on your name until you sign off.

  3. Your callers dial full days

    Dedicated setters call your territory 7 to 8 hours a day, handle objections, qualify the homeowner, and book the appointment. We coach and QA every day.

  4. Appointments hit your calendar

    Every qualified appointment lands directly on your calendar, and you watch the whole operation on a live dashboard. Scale by adding agents when you want more volume.

Straight Answers

Cold calling services,
what owners ask first

What are cold calling services?

Cold calling services are a done-for-you outbound program where an outside team dials prospects on your behalf and books appointments. With us, you get dedicated cold callers assigned only to your territory. They are recruited, trained, and managed by Premura, and every appointment they set lands directly in your calendar.

Are the cold callers dedicated to my company or shared?

Dedicated. Your cold callers work exclusively on your campaign and your territory, not a shared pool rotating between clients. You pick the zip codes, you approve the script, and the same agents learn your offer over time instead of reading it cold every day.

How many appointments will a cold caller book?

We manage to roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. Setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day against fresh data, so volume scales with how many dedicated agents you run. Real client results are on our reviews page, from 8 to 10 installs a month up to 50 to 60 appointments a month.

Do you provide the calling data and the script?

Yes. We run in-house data and scraping software refreshed weekly, so your callers are always working current lists in your zip codes. Scripts and QA are built by us and approved by you, then refined with daily coaching so the pitch keeps improving.

How much do cold calling services cost?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, because it depends on how many callers you run and for how long. There is no long-term lock-in required to start, so you can prove the model before you scale.
