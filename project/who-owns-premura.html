What The Company Does

What Premura Corp
actually builds and runs

Knowing who owns a company matters, but so does knowing what they do all day. Here is the work, in plain terms.

  1. Builds and manages dedicated call centers

    Premura builds and runs in-house call centers for solar, roofing, and home-services companies. More than 160 trained agents work across the floors, calling on behalf of clients.

    Agents are recruited, trained, and managed by Premura, not subcontracted out.

  2. Cold-calls the client's own territory

    Each client's agents call that client's zip codes exclusively, using in-house data and scraping software refreshed weekly. Setters dial seven to eight hours a day.

    Dedicated, not shared, so the appointments belong to one client only.

  3. Books qualified appointments into your calendar

    The output is around one qualified appointment per agent per day, booked straight into the client's calendar, with scripts and QA the client approves and a live dashboard to watch it happen.

    Daily coaching and management by Premura, with no long-term lock-in to start.
