    You show up to sell. We keep the calendar full.

Straight Answers

Virtual appointment setters
what everyone asks first

What is a virtual appointment setter?

A virtual appointment setter is a remote agent who cold-calls prospects in your territory and books qualified appointments into your calendar. With Premura the setter is dedicated to your account, not shared across clients, and is recruited, trained, and managed by us so you do not run the hiring or the daily floor yourself.

Are the appointment setters dedicated to my business or shared?

Dedicated. Each setter works your territory, dials your approved script, and books to your calendar only. They do not split their day across other companies. You pick the zip codes and the appointments belong to you.

How many appointments can a virtual appointment setter book?

Setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day and we manage toward roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. Real client results vary, from Caesar at 8 to 10 solar installs a month to Mitchell Rossey at 50 to 60 appointments a month across a larger team. You can watch operators describe their own numbers on our reviews page.

Do I have to hire, train, or manage the setters?

No. Premura recruits, trains, and manages the setters, runs daily coaching, and monitors campaigns on a live dashboard you can open any time. You approve the script and the QA standards. We handle the floor.

How much do virtual appointment setters cost?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, because it depends on how many setters you run and for how long. There is no long-term lock-in to start the conversation.
