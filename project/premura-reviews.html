What Clients Report

What people actually say
about working with Premura

Premura is a paid, done-for-you service. We build and manage a dedicated call center for you, so the feedback that matters is not about software, it is about whether the appointments show up and whether the operation is run well.

The recurring themes

  • Agents are dedicated to one client, calling that client's territory with that client's script
  • Qualified appointments land directly on the closer's calendar, not in a spreadsheet
  • Clients watch the floor on a live dashboard rather than waiting for a monthly report
  • The team is recruited, trained, and managed by Premura, so the client is not running a second company

What we will not claim

  • No aggregate star score, because we did not want to publish a number we controlled
  • No "trusted by 500 companies" style stats we cannot show you the source for
  • No guaranteed results, because outcomes depend on your offer, territory, and close rate
  • Instead, named clients on camera and a public Better Business Bureau profile

Roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day is the rhythm we manage toward. Your mileage varies with market and offer, and the honest way to gauge fit is to watch the videos and talk to us directly.
