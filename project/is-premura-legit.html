How The Service Actually Works

Nothing hidden about
how the work gets done

Legitimacy is not only about being registered. It is about doing what you say. Here is the plain version.

  1. Dedicated agents, exclusive to your territory

    Your agents are not shared across a pool of companies. They are dedicated to you, calling your territory with your script, booking into your calendar. Premura recruits, trains, and manages them.

  2. Appointments booked straight into your calendar

    Agents cold-call and qualify, then set appointments directly on your closers' calendars. A realistic pace is around one qualified appointment per agent per day.

  3. A live dashboard you can open any time

    You do not wait for a monthly report. You watch results on a live dashboard, which is exactly the kind of transparency a business hiding something would never offer.

  4. No long-term lock-in to start

    You are not forced into a multi-year contract to begin. That lowers the risk of finding out whether the service works for your company.
