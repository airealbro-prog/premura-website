What Gets Set Up

Four moving parts,
configured and maintained for you

A dialer subscription is the easy purchase. The work is the data, the numbers, and the integration behind it. Premura sets up and runs all of it.

  • The auto-dialer. Configured per campaign so your dedicated agents move through your territory efficiently instead of hand-dialing. No seat for you to license or maintain.
  • Local numbers. Calls come from local numbers, which homeowners are far more likely to answer than an unfamiliar out-of-area line.
  • In-house data, refreshed weekly. Our own scraping software builds and refreshes contact data every week, filtered to the zip codes you pick, so the dialer is always fed fresh territory-specific lists.
  • CRM and calendar integration. The dialer and CRM connect to your calendar, so qualified appointments book straight onto your closers' schedules and every touch is tracked.
How It Works

From zip codes to a
running dial in four steps

You do not touch a settings panel. Here is how the stack goes live.

  1. You pick the territory

    Choose the zip codes you want called. Coverage is US-wide and everything downstream is filtered to your market.

    Your territory defines the data set.

  2. We build and load the data

    Our in-house scraping software pulls contact data for your zip codes and refreshes it weekly, so the dialer never runs on a stale list.

    Fresh data every week, not a one-time list purchase.

  3. We configure the dialer and numbers

    The auto-dialer is set up per campaign with local numbers, retry logic, and your approved script, ready for your dedicated agents to start calling.

    Configured for you, part of the package, not a separate subscription.

  4. Bookings sync to your calendar

    The CRM and calendar integration sends qualified appointments straight to your closers, and you watch dials, contacts, and bookings on a live dashboard.

    You see the whole operation without running any of it.

Straight Answers

Dialer setup services
what everyone asks first

What do dialer setup services include?

With Premura the dialer setup is part of the managed service. We configure the auto-dialer, provision local numbers, load in-house data that is scraped and refreshed weekly, and integrate the CRM and your calendar. You do not buy a dialer seat or wire up the stack yourself.

Do I need to buy my own dialer software?

No. The dialer, the numbers, and the calling data come with the service. Premura runs the stack for the dedicated agents working your territory, so there is no separate dialer subscription for you to purchase, license, or maintain.

Where does the calling data come from?

Premura uses in-house data and scraping software that is refreshed weekly, filtered to the zip codes you choose. Fresh, territory-specific data feeds the dialer so agents spend their 7 to 8 hours a day calling your market, not chasing stale lists.

Does the dialer connect to my CRM and calendar?

Yes. The dialer and CRM are integrated with your calendar so qualified appointments book straight onto your closers' schedules, and every dial, contact, and booking shows up on a live dashboard you can open any time.

How much do dialer setup services cost?

The dialer and data stack are included in the managed service rather than priced as a separate line item. Premura prices per dedicated agent, discussed on a call, with no long-term lock-in to start the conversation.
