How It Works

From your zip codes
to booked appointments

The mechanics are simple on your side because the complicated part lives on ours. Here is the path from signing up to a full calendar.

  • You pick the territory. Choose the zip codes and the market you want worked. Coverage is nationwide across the US, and your agents call only your area.
  • We staff and train the team. Dedicated agents are assigned, trained on your offer, and briefed on the qualification bar you set. No hiring or turnover lands on your desk.
  • Setters dial your data daily. Seven to eight hours a day against in-house data refreshed weekly, following the script and QA you approved.
  • Qualified appointments hit your calendar. Every booking lands directly on your closers' calendars, tracked and visible on your live dashboard.
  • You scale by adding agents. One agent or five. Add capacity when the pipeline is working, with no long-term lock-in required to start.

The honest cut: if you plan to run outbound calling as a permanent, large in-house department, building your own eventually makes sense. If what you want this quarter is qualified appointments on the calendar, an outsourced call center company that already runs the floor gets you there faster. Straight Answers

Outbound call center services,
questions we get first

What are outbound call center services?

Outbound call center services mean a team of agents that dials out on your behalf to reach prospects and book appointments, rather than waiting for inbound calls. Premura provides the agents, the dialer, the calling data, the scripts, and the daily management as one package, so you get booked appointments without recruiting or running a floor yourself.

Are the agents dedicated to my company or shared?

Dedicated. Each agent calls for one client and works only your territory and your zip codes. They are not a shared pool splitting time across accounts. You approve the script and qualification criteria, and you can talk to your agents directly.

How many appointments does an outbound agent produce?

We manage to roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. Setters dial seven to eight hours a day against fresh data, and you scale volume by adding agents rather than by re-hiring in-house. Actual numbers vary by offer, territory, and season.

Do I need to provide the phone list and dialer?

No. Premura uses in-house data and scraping software that is refreshed weekly, and the dialer is configured and included. You pick the zip codes you want worked and approve the script. We handle recruiting, training, tooling, and daily coaching.

How much do outbound call center services cost?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, because it depends on how many agents you run and the length of the campaign. There is no long-term lock-in required to start, and you scale up or down by adding or removing agents.
