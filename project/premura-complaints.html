The Objections We Hear Most

Every concern worth raising,
answered without spin

A paid, done-for-you service should be able to face its own objections in public. Here are ours.

  1. "Results take too long to show up"

    This is a fair concern, and the honest answer is that a campaign needs a ramp period. Agents have to learn your territory, your offer, and the objections specific to your market. Some clients see appointments in the first days, but judging fairly means giving it a few weeks. The baseline we manage to is about one qualified appointment per dedicated agent per day.

    What we do about it: campaigns are monitored daily from day one, so problems get caught during the ramp, not after a wasted month.

  2. "How do I know the agents are any good?"

    Agents are recruited, trained, and managed by Premura, and they are dedicated to your territory rather than shared across clients. They are coached, tested, and replaced until they perform. You are not asked to take quality on faith.

    What we do about it: you watch every call outcome on a live dashboard and talk to your agents directly, so quality is visible.

  3. "Am I locked into a long contract?"

    No long-term lock-in is required to start. You can begin the conversation without signing a multi-year commitment, and you scale by adding or removing dedicated agents rather than being trapped in a fixed term.

    What we do about it: the model is built to earn the next month, not to bind you to it.

  4. "I am worried about the cost"

    Pricing is per dedicated agent and depends on how many agents you run and for how long. We do not post a single headline number because the right team size is different for every client, and a number without context tends to mislead. The price of an agent includes the trained agent, the configured technology, and daily management.

    What we do about it: on a call we size the team honestly, one agent or five, against what you can realistically close.

  5. "What if I am unhappy once I start?"

    Tell us directly and early. Because campaigns are managed daily, most issues can be diagnosed and adjusted quickly, whether that means coaching an agent, tightening the script, or changing how the territory is worked.

    What we do about it: email support@premura.org, raise it with your account contact, or point to what the dashboard is showing. Direct communication is the fastest path to a fix.
