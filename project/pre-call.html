Every Answer, Before We Talk

17 questions operators ask us
— answered on video

Tap any question to watch a short answer from Michael or Matt. The written version is right underneath — but the video has the detail. Come to the call with the ones you still want to dig into.

01What's the cost, minimum build, and is there a cap to scaling?

Short answer: Two-agent minimum (it's dialer math, not an upsell), roughly a $5k/month floor, and most clients land at $50–$100 per qualified appointment once ramped. Scaling only caps at your closers' capacity and territory. ▶ Michael breaks down the full numbers in the video.

02Does it work without door-to-door or paid ads?

Short answer: Yes — it's your own internal cold-calling engine: your setters, your dialer, your data, booking straight to your calendar. No knocking, no ads. ▶ Full explanation in the video.

03Am I a fit if I'm under 5 installs a month?

Short answer: Honestly, probably not yet — a call center needs calendar capacity for 40–50 appointments, closing experience, and buildout budget. If that's you, we'd love to talk. ▶ Michael explains who it's really for.

04Who must be on the call?

Short answer: Everyone who'll manage, operate, or pay for the call center. If a decision-maker is missing, we can't run the full numbers build. ▶ Why, in about a minute.

05How fast are we live, and what happens in the first 14 days?

Short answer: Usually 1–3 weeks: three rounds of interviews (you pick the hires), we scrape your zip-code data, set up the dialer and local numbers, then a launch call. ▶ Full walkthrough in the video.

06Where can you run this?

Short answer: Anywhere in the US — Alaska and Hawaii included. You pick the exact zip codes and targeting filters. ▶ More from Matt in the video.

07Who hires, trains, manages, and QAs the appointments day to day?

Short answer: We do — recruiting, screening, training, dialer, data, daily coaching, and QA against your checklist. You get a dashboard with full control to hire, fire, or change anything. ▶ See how it works in the video.

08What exactly is a "qualified appointment," and how are handoffs done?

Short answer: You define every qualifying question and targeting criterion; we book only appointments that meet them, decision-makers present — pushed to your CRM, dashboard, text, email, and Discord. ▶ Full definition in the video.

09What results are clients seeing, and what's the show rate?

Short answer: Expect ~1 qualified appointment per agent per day (20–25/month), 45–65% show rates, clients closing 25–70%. We confirm both decision-makers, call day-of, and re-inject every no-show. ▶ Michael's full answer in the video.

10Can I see scripts, QA standards, and reporting before launch?

Short answer: Yes — you review and sign off on scripts, objection handling, the qualification rubric, and report templates before go-live. ▶ Examples inside the video.

11What data do you call, and how do you handle DNC / brand protection?

Short answer: We built $100k+ of in-house scraping software tuned for home improvement — age, roof size, power usage, even AI credit prediction — refreshed weekly. ▶ Matt covers DNC and brand protection in the video.

12What's a setter's daily activity, and how do we ramp a campaign?

Short answer: Setters dial 7–8 hours a day — up to 4,000 dials and 200–300 live conversations each — and yes, you can start small and ramp up. ▶ Full numbers in the video.

13How do you coordinate territories to avoid double-booking?

Short answer: Our software links to each closer's calendar and books with 100% accuracy across every territory — no double-booking, even at massive scale. ▶ How it works, in the video.

14Can we pause or surge for weather, crews, or inventory?

Short answer: Yes — add agents, cut agents, pause, or resume any time, for weather, crew availability, inventory, whatever you need. ▶ The playbook is in the video.

15How do you handle saturated territories — and can I storm chase?

Short answer: We rotate zip codes so territories never burn (4 years proven in South Florida), keep crews busy through snow, and can blitz a hot storm area within about an hour. ▶ Michael explains in the video.

16If performance dips, what levers do you pull and how fast?

Short answer: We find the weakest link — volume, booking, show, or close — and pull the right lever: agents, script, data, numbers, dialer speed, retraining. Daily contact, weekly meetings. ▶ Full list in the video.

17What do top clients do — and what mistakes kill month one?

Short answer: Winners put their best closers on it, give feedback, dedicate 1–2 hours a week, run 6+ agents, and confirm every appointment. The mistake that kills month one is expecting peak performance during ramp-up. ▶ Michael's playbook in the video.
