01What's the cost, minimum build, and is there a cap to scaling?

Short: Two-agent minimum (dialer math, not an upsell), ~$5k/mo floor, most land at $50–$100 per qualified appointment once ramped. Scaling only caps at your closers' capacity. ▶ Michael breaks down the numbers.

02Does it work without door-to-door or paid ads?

Short: Yes — your own cold-calling engine: your setters, your dialer, your data, booking straight to your calendar. No knocking, no ads. ▶ Full answer in the video.

03Am I a fit if I'm under 5 installs a month?

Short: Honestly, probably not yet — you need calendar capacity for 40–50 appointments, closing experience, and buildout budget. If that's you, let's talk. ▶ Michael on who it's really for.

04Who must be on the call?

Short: Everyone who'll manage, run, or pay for the call center. Missing a decision-maker means we can't run your real numbers. ▶ Why, in a minute.

05How fast are we live, and what happens in the first 14 days?

Short: 1–3 weeks: three rounds of interviews (you pick the hires), we scrape your zip-code data, set up dialer + local numbers, then a launch call. ▶ Full walkthrough.

06Where can you run this?

Short: Anywhere in the US — Alaska and Hawaii included. You pick the exact zips and filters. ▶ More from Matt.

07Who hires, trains, manages, and QAs day to day?

Short: We do — recruiting, training, dialer, data, daily coaching, QA against your checklist. You get a dashboard with full control. ▶ See how it runs.

08What exactly is a "qualified appointment," and how are handoffs done?

Short: You define every qualifying question; we book only appointments that meet them, decision-makers present — pushed to your CRM, dashboard, text, email, Discord. ▶ Full definition.

09What results are clients seeing, and what's the show rate?

Short: ~1 qualified appointment per agent per day (20–25/mo), 45–65% show rates, clients closing 25–70%. We confirm both decision-makers and re-inject no-shows. ▶ Michael's full answer.

10Can I see scripts, QA standards, and reporting before launch?

Short: Yes — you review and sign off on scripts, objection handling, the qualification rubric, and report templates before go-live. ▶ Examples inside.

11What data do you call, and how do you handle DNC / brand protection?

Short: $100k+ of in-house scraping software tuned for home improvement — age, roof size, power usage, even AI credit prediction — refreshed weekly. ▶ Matt on DNC + brand protection.

12What's a setter's daily activity, and how do we ramp?

Short: Setters dial 7–8 hours a day — up to 4,000 dials, 200–300 live conversations each — and yes, you can start small and ramp. ▶ Full numbers.

13How do you coordinate territories to avoid double-booking?

Short: Our software links to each closer's calendar and books with 100% accuracy across every territory — no double-booking, even at scale. ▶ How it works.

14Can we pause or surge for weather, crews, or inventory?

Short: Yes — add agents, cut agents, pause, or resume any time, for whatever you need. ▶ The playbook.

15How do you handle saturated territories — and can I storm chase?

Short: We rotate zips so territories never burn (4 yrs proven in South Florida), keep crews busy through snow, and can blitz a storm area within about an hour. ▶ Michael explains.

16If performance dips, what levers do you pull and how fast?

Short: We find the weakest link — volume, booking, show, or close — and pull the right lever: agents, script, data, numbers, dialer speed, retraining. Daily contact, weekly meetings. ▶ Full list.

17What do top clients do — and what mistakes kill month one?

Short: Winners put their best closers on it, give feedback, dedicate 1–2 hrs/week, run 6+ agents, and confirm every appointment. The killer mistake is expecting peak performance during ramp. ▶ Michael's playbook.
