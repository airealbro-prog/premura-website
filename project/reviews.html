Is Premura legit?

Yes — and you shouldn't take our word for it. Premura Corp is a registered Florida corporation, incorporated September 2022 and headquartered in Medley, FL. We hold an A rating with the Better Business Bureau, we employ 160+ trained agents, and every client result we publish is on video with a real name attached.

The best way to verify us is the way our clients did: book a free strategy call. You'll see the live dashboards, meet the process, and get territory-level specifics before you spend a dollar. No contracts are required to start the conversation.

What about the complaints online?

If you search around, you may find a small number of negative posts written by former sales agents — not by clients. Most trace back to a payment dispute with one agent. We took the criticism seriously anyway: we rebuilt our agent payment process end-to-end, with a published pay schedule and faster payout timelines, because 160+ people rely on it. We won't pretend online criticism doesn't exist — we'd rather you read it, then ask us about it directly on a call. We'll answer anything.
