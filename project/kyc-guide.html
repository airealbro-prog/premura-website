Form Guide

How to fill out the KYC form

A field-by-field walkthrough of the Premura KYC form. It takes about five minutes to complete. Work through the fields in the order they appear below.

Before you start, have these to hand:
  • Your business formation documents (for the exact registered name and entity type)
  • Your business licence number, if your state issues one
  • The full physical address of the business
  • The name and title of whoever is authorised to sign on the company's behalf

Your contact details

Who we should reach out to about this submission.

1 First Name

Your own first name — the person filling in this form. This does not need to be the same person who signs the agreement.

ExampleVanisha

2 Last Name

Your own last name.

ExampleKumar

3 Phone Required

The best number to reach you on, including the country code. Mobile is usually best.

Example+1 (555) 014-2277

4 Email Required

Your email address. Double-check the spelling — anything we send back about this form goes to this address.

Examplevanisha@vanishahomes.com

Your business

Copy these straight from your formation documents so the spelling matches exactly.

5 KYC Business Legal Name

The exact registered name of your business as it appears on your formation documents, including the suffix. This is not your brand or trading name.

ExampleVanisha Real Estate LLC

6 KYC DBA Name

Your "doing business as" name — the name customers actually see, if it differs from the legal name above. If you trade under your legal name, enter the same name again.

ExampleVanisha Homes

7 KYC Entity Type

How the business is registered. Use the wording on your formation paperwork — for example LLC, S-Corporation, C-Corporation, Partnership or Sole Proprietorship.

ExampleLLC

8 KYC Business Address

The full physical address of the business: street, city, state and ZIP. Don't leave any part out, and avoid a PO box if you have a street address.

Example1420 Oak Street, Kent, WA 98030

9 KYC Website

Your business website, including the https:// at the front. If you don't have one, enter None rather than leaving it blank.

Examplehttps://vanishahomes.com

10 KYC License Number

Your contractor or business licence number for the state you operate in. If your state doesn't issue one for your trade, enter N/A rather than leaving it blank.

ExampleWA-CC-0198432

Who is signing

The person with authority to sign on behalf of the business.

11 KYC Signer Name

The full name of the person authorised to sign agreements for the business. This may or may not be you.

ExampleVanisha Kumar

12 KYC Signer Title

That person's role in the business.

ExampleOwner  ·  Managing Member  ·  President

Where you work

13 KYC States Served

The states you take work in. Separate several with commas, and write them out in full rather than abbreviating. If you operate everywhere, enter Nationwide.

ExampleWashington, Oregon, Idaho

Finishing up

14 Tick the consent boxes

Just above the Submit button there are two small checkboxes. Tick both before submitting — the form won't go through without them.

15 Submit

Press Submit. The page will confirm once it has gone through. If it doesn't, scroll back up and look for a field highlighted in red.

A few things that trip people up:
  • Legal name and DBA name are different fields — the legal name is the registered one, with the LLC or Inc. suffix.
  • If something genuinely doesn't apply, write N/A instead of leaving it empty.
  • Spell the business name exactly as it appears on your documents, including punctuation.
  • The signer must be someone with authority to sign for the business.
