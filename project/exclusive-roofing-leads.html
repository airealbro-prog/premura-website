The Calendar Problem

Storm season is a windfall
It's not a business model

Referrals slow down. Storms miss your county. The crews still need work and the overhead still bills monthly. A dedicated calling operation is how the inspection calendar stops depending on the weather.

  • Year-round pipeline. Agents dial your territory every working day — retail roof replacements, aging shingles, insurance questions — not just post-storm panic.
  • Storms multiply it, not enable it. When weather does hit your area, the same machine that was already booking inspections simply converts at a higher rate.
  • You choose the territory. Your agents work the neighborhoods and counties you want to grow in — not wherever a lead vendor happens to have inventory this week.
  • Scale with the season. Add agents ahead of your busy months, run leaner in the slow ones. Pricing is per agent, so capacity is a dial you control.

Straight Answers

Exclusive-lead questions
roofers ask us first

Are the leads actually exclusive?

Yes — structurally, not as a policy promise. Your agent is dedicated to your company: they call only your territory and book only your calendar. The appointment never exists in a marketplace, so there's nothing to resell. Shared roofing leads are commonly sold to 3–4 contractors at once.

Is this contact info, or booked appointments?

Booked appointments. A qualified homeowner agrees to a specific inspection time in a live conversation, and it lands on your closer's calendar with live sync. You're not buying a list to chase.

Does it only work during storm season?

No — that's the point. The calendar fills year-round from daily dialing; storms make the same machine more productive rather than being the only thing that switches it on.

What does it cost versus buying leads?

Pricing is per dedicated agent — averaging $2,500 per agent per month — with 5+ qualified appointments per agent per week as the floor. That's roughly $115 per exclusive booked inspection, versus shared leads that look cheaper per record but get resold to your competitors and still need chasing and setting.
