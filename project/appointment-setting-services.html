Appointments vs Qualified Appointments

A full calendar is worthless
if the appointments are junk

Plenty of services will fill your calendar. The number that matters is how many of those appointments turn into a real conversation with someone who fits your offer. That is the entire point of appointment setting, and where most vendors quietly fail.

Volume-only appointments

What cheap dialing produces

  • Anyone who answers gets booked to hit a quota
  • Your closers burn hours on unfit or no-show calls
  • No shared criteria, so quality swings week to week
  • The calendar looks busy while close rate falls

Premura qualified appointments

What a managed setting team produces

  • Homeowner is qualified against criteria you approve
  • Only fit appointments reach your closers' calendars
  • Same dedicated setters, so quality holds and improves
  • Daily coaching keeps the bar where you set it

Setting the appointment is the easy half. Setting an appointment your closer is glad to keep is the job, and it takes a script you approve plus daily management to protect the standard.

How It Works

Four steps to appointments
landing on your calendar

A clear path from your territory to booked, qualified appointments. The full breakdown lives on how it works.

  1. Define the territory and the fit

    You choose the zip codes and tell us what a good appointment looks like. We turn that into qualification criteria and load fresh data for your area.

    In-house scraping software refreshed weekly keeps the lists current in your zip codes.

  2. Approve the script

    We write the opener, qualification questions, and the booking close, then you approve it. The criteria that make an appointment qualified are built in before a single call goes out.

    Scripts and QA are ours to build and yours to approve, then refined every campaign.

  3. Dedicated setters do the work

    Your assigned setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day, qualify homeowners against your criteria, and book the ones who fit. We coach and QA them daily so the standard holds.

    Roughly one qualified appointment per setter per day is the number we manage to.

  4. It lands on your calendar

    Each qualified appointment syncs straight into your calendar in real time, and you track everything on a live dashboard. Add setters whenever you want more appointments.

    Live calendar sync and a live dashboard, so nothing sits in a spreadsheet.

Straight Answers

Appointment setting services,
what owners ask first

What does an appointment setting company do?

An appointment setting company calls prospects, qualifies them against your criteria, and books them onto your calendar so your closers only talk to interested homeowners. We assign dedicated setters to your territory, manage them daily, and sync every booked appointment directly into your calendar.

What makes an appointment qualified?

Qualified means the homeowner fits the criteria you approve before a slot is booked, so your closers are not sitting through unfit calls. We build the qualification questions into the script you sign off on, and coach setters daily to hold that bar rather than book anyone who answers.

How do the appointments reach my calendar?

Every qualified appointment is booked straight into your calendar in real time, and you watch dials, contacts, and bookings on a live dashboard. There is no spreadsheet handoff and no waiting for an end-of-day report, so your team can prepare for each appointment as it lands.

Do I keep control of the script and territory?

Yes. You pick the zip codes your setters work, and you approve the script and qualification criteria before anything goes live. Your setters are dedicated to your campaign, so they learn your offer instead of reading it cold between other clients.

How is appointment setting priced?

Pricing is per dedicated setter and is discussed on a call, since it depends on how many setters you run and for how long. There is no long-term lock-in required to begin, so you can validate the appointment flow before scaling the team up.
