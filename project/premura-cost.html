Premura Pricing

How much does Premura cost?
An honest answer, without a fake number

Here is the straight version. Premura is priced per dedicated agent, and the exact figure is quoted on a call once we know your territory and team size. We are not going to slap a random number on this page to win the click. Instead, this page explains exactly what you are paying for and what moves the price, so you walk into that call already informed.

The Honest Reason

Why there is no sticker price
on this page

A one-agent solar campaign in one metro and a five-agent roofing campaign across a state are different builds. Any single number printed here would either mislead the smaller operator or be padded to cover the biggest case. So we quote it on a call, scoped to the team you actually need. That is not a sales tactic. It is the only way to give you a figure that is honest.

What we will never do: publish a teaser price and then reveal a bigger one later. The number you get on the call is the number, sized to your build.

What Moves the Price

Two things drive
what Premura costs

Pricing is per dedicated agent, so the two levers are simple and easy to reason about before you ever get on a call.

Number of agents

The single biggest factor

  • Every dedicated agent is recruited, trained, and managed by Premura
  • Each agent dials 7 to 8 hours a day, exclusive to your territory
  • More agents means more calendar coverage and more appointments booked
  • You can start with one agent and add more, rather than committing to a large team up front

Territory and campaign

The scope your agents work

  • You pick the zip codes; coverage is across the United States
  • Territory size and vertical, solar or roofing or home services, shape the build
  • In-house data and scraping software is refreshed weekly to keep the lists live
  • Scripts and QA are set up and approved for your specific campaign

The honest cut: if you only need appointments in one small territory, you do not pay for a floor of agents. If you want to blanket a state, you scale up. The price tracks the team, nothing hidden underneath it.

What Is Included

You are renting a running operation,
not a person on a phone

The reason we price per dedicated agent instead of per lead is that a Premura agent comes wrapped in an entire operation. Here is what the price actually covers.

  • A dedicated agent, exclusive to you. Not shared across a pool of companies. Your territory, your script, your calendar.
  • Recruiting, training, and daily management. Premura hires and trains the agent, coaches them daily, and replaces them until they perform. That is the piece most in-house builds underestimate.
  • The dialer and the tech stack. The configured dialer is part of the package, not a separate subscription you go buy and integrate.
  • In-house data and scraping software. Contact data is generated by Premura's own software and refreshed weekly, so agents are not dialing stale lists.
  • Scripts and QA you approve. You sign off on the script and the quality standards. Nothing runs on your name without your approval.
  • A live dashboard. You watch appointments and activity in real time, not in a monthly report.

Common questions

How much does Premura cost?

Premura is priced per dedicated agent, and the exact number is quoted on a call once we understand your territory and how many agents you need. We do not publish a single figure because a one-agent solar campaign and a five-agent roofing campaign are genuinely different builds. The call is where we size it honestly.

Why doesn't Premura list a price on the website?

Because a fair price depends on how many dedicated agents you run and the territory they cover, a single sticker number would either be misleading or padded to cover the biggest case. Instead we scope it with you on a call, so the quote matches the actual team you need rather than an average.

What drives the price up or down?

Two things mainly: the number of dedicated agents you run, and the territory those agents cover. More agents is more recruiting, training, and daily management. Beyond that, the price reflects a complete operation, not a single seat, so it does not change based on hidden add-ons.

What is included in the price?

A dedicated agent exclusive to your territory, plus recruiting, training, and daily management of that agent, the dialer, the in-house data and scraping software refreshed weekly, scripts and QA you approve, and a live dashboard. You are renting a running operation, not just a person on a phone.

Are there long-term contracts?

There is no long-term lock-in to start. That is deliberate, because it lowers the risk of finding out whether the service works for your company before committing to anything lengthy. Pricing and term are both part of the conversation on the call.

Is Premura worth the cost?

That depends on whether you can close the appointments a dedicated agent books. On the reviews page, operators describe results like 17 deals in a first 30 days and 8 to 10 installs a month. The math works when you have a sales team ready to run the appointments the call center fills.
