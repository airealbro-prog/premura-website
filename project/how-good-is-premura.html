The Number To Plan Around

What the model realistically
produces per agent

The headline numbers above come from clients running for a while, closing well, often on more than one agent. Here is the baseline metric we actually manage to.

The realistic baseline

Plan your business on this, not on the best case

  • About one qualified appointment per dedicated agent per day
  • Agents are dedicated to you, not shared across other clients
  • Each agent works your territory and your script exclusively
  • Campaigns are monitored daily, not reviewed once a month
  • You watch every result on a live dashboard you can open any time

What turns that into deals

The part you own

  • Closers who work the calendar the same day appointments land
  • An offer that already converts for someone in your market
  • Enough ramp time for agents to learn your territory and objections
  • Direct communication with your agents so feedback loops stay tight
  • Honest team sizing, one agent or five, based on how many appointments you can actually close

The honest cut: Premura fills the top of your funnel with qualified, scheduled conversations. The revenue depends on the parts of the machine you run. A full calendar is only worth what your closers do with it.

Where It Works, Where It Does Not

And who Premura
is honestly not for

A service that is good for everyone is usually good for no one. Here is the candid version.

Premura works well when

  • You sell solar, roofing, or a home service with a clear offer
  • You have closers ready to work appointments every day
  • You want appointments without building and managing a floor yourself
  • You can give the campaign a few weeks to ramp before you judge it
  • You want dedicated agents on your territory, not a shared lead pool

Premura is not for you if

  • You need deals this week with zero ramp period
  • Your offer has not converted for anyone yet
  • You cannot staff closers to follow up the same day
  • You want a guaranteed number of sales rather than qualified appointments
  • You are looking for the cheapest possible lead rather than a managed team

How good is Premura, really?

Premura builds and manages dedicated outbound call centers for solar, roofing, and home-services companies. Real clients have posted results on camera: Scott Kelly closed 17 deals in his first 30 days, Leicer closed 22 deals in 30 days, Jorge books 20 or more deals a month, and Mitchell Rossey runs 50 to 60 appointments a month. Those are strong outcomes, but they are not a guarantee. The realistic number we manage to is about one qualified appointment per dedicated agent per day.

What results are realistic with a Premura call center?

Plan around roughly one qualified appointment per dedicated agent per day. Some clients run well above that, and the video reviews show it, but your close rate then depends on your offer, your pricing, and how fast your closers work the calendar. We manage to the appointment number. What you do with the appointment is the part you own.

Are the testimonials real?

Yes. The reviews on our site are real operators talking through their own numbers on camera, with first names and the vertical they work in. You can watch them on the reviews page. We do not publish star ratings or invented review counts.

Who is Premura not a good fit for?

Premura is not a fit if you need appointments this week with zero ramp, if you cannot staff closers to work the calendar every day, or if your offer has not converted for anyone yet. Outbound fills the top of the funnel. It cannot fix a product or a sales process that is not already closing.

How is Premura managed day to day?

Agents are dedicated to your territory and exclusive to you, not shared across other clients. Premura recruits, trains, and manages them, monitors campaigns daily rather than in a monthly report, and gives you a live dashboard plus direct communication with your agents.
