What It Is

What a Premura
call center actually is

Not a lead list. Not a shared pool of callers dialing for a dozen companies at once. A working outbound floor, dedicated to you.

  1. Trained agents dialing your territory

    Premura recruits, screens, and trains the callers, then puts them on the phones against your territory. They cold-call, handle objections, qualify the homeowner, and book the appointment. You are renting a working team, not a piece of software.

    160+ trained agents already work across our floors.

  2. Appointments booked straight into your calendar

    A qualified appointment is only useful if it lands where your closers can work it. Premura books directly into your calendar, so the handoff from setter to closer is automatic instead of a spreadsheet someone forgets to check.

    As a benchmark, plan on about one qualified appointment per agent per day.

  3. Dedicated and exclusive to you

    Your agents work your territory, your script, and your calendar only. They are not split across other clients. That exclusivity is the difference between a call center and a shared lead vendor, and it is why the appointments feel like your own team's work.

    No long-term lock-in is required to start.

  4. Managed for you, visible to you

    Premura runs the floor day to day: recruiting, training, coaching, and replacing agents until they perform. You do not manage callers. You watch the results come in on a live dashboard you can open any time.

    Daily management is part of the service, not an add-on.

Straight Answers

Premura call centers,
common questions

What is a Premura call center?

A Premura call center is a team of dedicated, trained outbound agents that Premura recruits, staffs, and manages on your behalf. The agents cold-call your territory and book qualified appointments straight into your calendar. Premura is a paid, done-for-you service; the agents work exclusively on one client's territory rather than being shared across many companies.

Which industries does Premura serve?

Premura builds and manages call centers for solar, roofing, and home-services companies. The model is the same across verticals: dedicated agents cold-call the client's territory and set qualified appointments for the client's sales team.

Are the agents dedicated or shared?

Dedicated. Your agents are exclusive to your territory, your script, and your calendar. They are not shared across multiple clients, and there is no long-term lock-in required to start.

How many appointments can I expect?

As a working benchmark, plan on roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. Actual volume depends on your territory, offer, and how many dedicated agents you run. Clients watch results on a live dashboard rather than waiting for a monthly report.

How do I verify Premura is a real company?

Premura Corp is a Florida corporation founded in September 2022, based in Medley, FL 33166. You can reach the company at 1-833-366-8784 or support@premura.org, review its Better Business Bureau profile, and watch named clients describe their results on the reviews page.

Is Premura a good fit for every company?

No, and Premura is candid about that. It is built for solar, roofing, and home-services companies that have a real offer and closers ready to work booked appointments. If you cannot yet handle a fuller calendar, or you want to build and own the calling operation in-house, a done-for-you call center may not be the right fit.
