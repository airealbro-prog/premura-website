How A Solar Campaign Runs

From your zip codes
to a booked consultation

A solar appointment setting campaign is a repeatable pipeline, not a lead drop. Here is what happens once you sign on.

  1. You pick the territory

    You choose the zip codes you want worked. Coverage is US wide, and your setter calls only the area you named. Nobody else is dialing that list on your behalf.

    In-house data and scraping software builds the calling list and refreshes it weekly.

  2. We staff and train the setter

    Premura recruits, screens, trains and manages the agent assigned to you from a floor of 160 plus trained callers. Cold calling has heavy washout, so we hire deep to keep the ones who can handle a real solar objection.

    Daily coaching and QA keep the setter sharp instead of drifting after week one.

  3. You approve the script

    The pitch, the qualifying questions, and the standard for a valid appointment are yours to sign off on. The setter opens the solar conversation, handles objections, and confirms the homeowner meets your criteria before booking.

    Scripts refined across every solar campaign we have run, adapted to your offer.

  4. The appointment lands on your calendar

    A qualified consultation is booked as a specific time directly into your closers' calendar, not dropped into a spreadsheet to chase. Your closers wake up to a day of confirmed solar appointments.

    A live dashboard shows dials, contacts and booked appointments any time you open it.

The Honest Comparison

Setters vs door knocking
vs buying solar leads

Door knocking

Free to start, expensive to scale

  • Your best closers spend the day walking, not closing
  • Weather, daylight and permits cap how many doors get hit
  • Reps burn out and turn over, and the territory resets each time
  • No confirmed appointment at the end, just a maybe on a doorstep

Buying shared leads

Fast to buy, sold to your competitors too

  • The same homeowner is sold to several solar companies at once
  • You race four other reps to call a form fill first
  • Cost per lead climbs while contact rates fall
  • You own a name, not a booked time on a calendar

Dedicated Premura setter

Right if you want confirmed solar consultations

  • One agent, exclusive to your territory, calling only your zip codes
  • Seven to eight dialing hours a day, refreshed data behind every call
  • A qualified consultation booked straight onto your closers' calendar
  • Recruited, trained and managed by us, with no long-term lock-in to start

Straight Answers

Solar appointment setting
questions we hear first

What is solar appointment setting?

It is outbound calling with one purpose: getting qualified homeowners onto your closers' calendars. A dedicated agent works your territory, dials seven to eight hours a day, has the first conversation about going solar, qualifies the homeowner against criteria you set, and books a specific consultation time. Your closers spend their day closing instead of prospecting.

Are the solar appointment setters dedicated to my company?

Yes. Each agent is assigned to one client and calls only that client's territory. Your setter is not shared across a marketplace of solar companies, so the appointments they book are yours alone and are never resold. You pick the zip codes, you approve the script, and you talk to your agents directly.

How many solar appointments will a setter book?

The floor we manage to is about one qualified appointment per agent per day, roughly five or more qualified consultations per agent per week. Real clients have reported far more across a full team: Scott Kelly closed 17 deals in 30 days, Leicer reported 22 deals in 30 days, and Jorge runs at 20 or more deals a month. You can watch those operators state their own numbers on the reviews page.

Is cold calling better than door knocking or buying solar leads?

They solve different problems. Door knocking puts your closers in the field instead of in front of homeowners who already agreed to talk. Bought or shared solar leads are sold to several companies at once, so you compete for the same homeowner. A dedicated setter combines the exclusivity of your own territory with the leverage of seven to eight calling hours a day, and books a confirmed time rather than handing you a name to chase.

What does solar appointment setting cost?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and depends on how many setters you run and for how long. There is no long-term lock-in to start. The specific number is discussed on a strategy call once we have mapped your territory and sized the team, so we quote against a real plan rather than a generic rate.
