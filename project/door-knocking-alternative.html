The Cap on Canvassing

Three territories where
door-to-door never gets a swing

This isn't about rep effort. These homeowners are unreachable by canvassing as a matter of physics, language, and windshield time.

  • Gated communities. Your reps physically can't enter — and gated neighborhoods skew toward exactly the home values solar closers want on the calendar. The phone doesn't stop at the gatehouse.
  • Spanish-speaking households. Most door teams can't hold a substantive conversation in Spanish, so millions of homeowners get a pamphlet at best. Our campaigns run with native bilingual setters who qualify and book in the homeowner's language.
  • Rural routes. Twenty minutes of windshield time between doors kills canvassing economics. A dialer covers a rural county as fast as a suburb — no rep will ever drive that route, and no rep has to.
  • And the quiet fourth: attrition. Door teams churn constantly — recruiting, training, and losing reps is a full-time job you re-do every season. A managed phone floor absorbs that churn for you; agents are trained, tested, and replaced until they perform.

Dedicated phone team

  • Live in 14 days, alongside your door team or instead of it

The operators doing this best don't pick a side — they knock where knocking works and let a phone team own everything behind the gates, past the county line, and en español. The phone half is the one that never quits mid-season.

Straight Answers

Questions door-first operators
ask us on every call

Is phone setting actually better than door knocking?

They cover different ground. D2D works where density, access, and rep quality line up. It physically can't enter gated communities, mostly skips Spanish-speaking households, and won't drive rural routes — a phone team reaches all three and books the appointments onto your calendar. Many operators run both.

Do your agents speak Spanish?

Yes — campaigns can run with native bilingual setters, opening the Spanish-speaking households most door teams never have a real conversation with.

Can we keep our door team and add this?

That's the most common setup. Your campaign is live within 14 days — doors keep the dense open neighborhoods, your agents own the gated, rural, and Spanish-speaking territory.

What does it cost next to a D2D program?

An average of $2,500 per dedicated agent per month — agent, dialer, CRM, and daily management included — with 5+ qualified appointments a week as the managed floor.
