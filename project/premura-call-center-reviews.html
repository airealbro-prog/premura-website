What Actually Gets Reviewed

Four things clients judge
about the call center service

A full calendar is the outcome. These four inputs are what a fair review of the service is really measuring, and they are where a managed floor either earns its keep or does not.

  1. Agent quality

    Cold calling has brutal washout, so the question is not whether someone can dial, it is whether they can hold a real objection and still book the slot. Premura recruits, screens, and trains agents, then keeps testing and replacing until the ones on your campaign perform.

    More than 160 trained agents across our floors, each dedicated to a single client's territory.

  2. Day-to-day management

    This is the piece most operators underestimate. A call center decays without someone listening to calls, coaching, and cutting what does not work. A weekly check-in is not management. Premura monitors campaigns daily.

    Agents are trained, tested, and managed by us, so you are not running a second company.

  3. Appointment quality

    Padded appointments waste your closer's day. Agents qualify the homeowner first, then book directly onto the closer's calendar so nothing gets lost in a spreadsheet. The rhythm we manage toward is about one qualified appointment per agent per day.

    Qualified, calendar-synced bookings, not raw lead lists dumped over the fence.

  4. The dashboard

    You should not have to wait for a monthly report to know how the floor is doing. Clients watch results on a live dashboard, so dials, connects, and appointments are visible while the campaign runs, not weeks later.

    A live view you can open any time, plus direct communication with your agents.

Straight Answers

Call center reviews.
What operators ask

What do call center reviews of Premura focus on?

Beyond deal counts, clients evaluate the service itself: whether the agents are competent on the phone, whether the floor is actually managed day to day, whether the appointments are qualified rather than padded, and whether the live dashboard gives an honest view of what is happening. Those are the four things this page walks through.

Are Premura agents dedicated or shared?

Dedicated. Each agent is exclusive to one client's territory, calling that client's list with that client's script and booking into that client's calendar. Agents are not split across multiple companies, which is a common complaint with shared or pay-per-lead call centers. Premura runs more than 160 trained agents across its floors.

How is appointment quality controlled?

Agents qualify the homeowner before booking, and the appointment drops straight onto the closer's calendar rather than into a spreadsheet. The target rhythm is roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. Because campaigns are monitored daily, weak lists and weak talk tracks get caught quickly rather than at the end of the month.

Who manages the floor day to day?

Premura does. The agents are recruited, trained, tested, and managed by us, and campaigns are reviewed daily rather than in a monthly summary. Clients do not run the floor themselves. You communicate with your agents directly and watch results on a live dashboard you can open any time.

Can I see the operation before I commit?

Yes. On a strategy call we will show you the kind of live dashboard current clients run on, walk through how the floor is staffed and managed, and size the team honestly.
