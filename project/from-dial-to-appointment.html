The Loop

What Happens to One Lead

Five steps, in order, every time. Nothing reaches your closer that skipped one.

  1. 01 STEP 01 · THE DIAL

    The dialer hands over a live homeowner

    Your agent works inside ReadyMode, a predictive dialer. It places the calls, eats the ring-outs and the voicemails, and connects your agent to live homeowners back-to-back instead of leaving them listening to dial tones. A dedicated agent spends seven to eight hours a day on the phone.

    The list underneath is yours. You pick the zip codes you want worked, and Premura's in-house data software feeds each agent fresh contact lists that are refreshed weekly. Calling runs under TCPA and Do-Not-Call rules.

  2. 02 STEP 02 · THE CONVERSATION

    A trained human opens the conversation

    Not a form fill, not a bot — a live conversation with a real person. Your agent has prior solar or roofing setting experience and works from a script you approved during setup, on the objections specific to your vertical.

    Every call is recorded and searchable, so the talk track is something you can audit rather than something you take on trust. Weak talk tracks get caught in days, because campaigns are watched daily rather than summarised monthly.

  3. 03 STEP 03 · FOUR POINT QUALIFICATION

    Four Point Qualification

    You define what counts as qualified, and your agent screens to that bar on every single call before anything gets booked. The criteria are built into the script and into QA during setup. Four checks have to clear.

    Qualification · your bar 4 of 4 required
    ✓ Homeowner Is the person on the phone the owner of the property?
    ✓ Roof or bill Roof age and condition, or the size of the power bill, against the numbers you set.
    ✓ Decision-maker present Will whoever signs be in the room for the appointment?
    ✓ Timeframe Are they looking inside a window your team can actually sell into?

    If any one of the four is missing, it does not get booked. That is the difference between an appointment and a lead.

  4. 04 STEP 04 · THE BOOKING

    The time is agreed while the agent is still on the phone

    This is what confirmed means here. A qualified homeowner agrees to a specific time in the live conversation — not a callback, not a maybe, not a form they might fill in later.

    Your Google Calendar is synced both ways, so the agent can only offer slots that are genuinely open, and double-bookings are not possible. Your closers get pinged the moment the appointment is set.

    Your closer walks in already knowing who they are meeting

    The appointment lands in GoHighLevel carrying the name, the phone number, the address and every qualification answer — reachable from any device. Every call, note and answer stays on the record and is auditable.

    You watch the whole floor on a live dashboard instead of waiting for a monthly report, and you, your agents and Premura's management team sit in one private channel. The appointment is exclusively yours and is never resold.

The Economics

What That Appointment Costs You

The same appointment, bought four different ways. These are the figures from Premura's own call center breakdown.

  Premura call center Door to door Paid ads Purchased appointments
Cost per appointment $31 — $300 $300
Cost per sale or install $250 – $500 $2,500 $2,000 $2,000
Your training load 5 hrs / week 15 hrs / week — —

$31 against $300. That is the number worth holding on to: roughly a tenth of what the same appointment costs through paid ads or through buying appointments from someone else — and a bought appointment is often not exclusive, so the same homeowner can be sold to more than one company. Yours never is.

The Rhythm

Now Multiply It by a Shift

That loop is one lead. Here is the pace Premura manages a dedicated agent to — not a best case, the working benchmark.

7–8 hours a day each agent spends dialing
~1 qualified appointment per agent, per day
5+ qualified consultations per agent, per week

Volume scales by adding dedicated agents, not by re-entering the hiring market yourself. Actual numbers depend on your territory, your offer and how many agents you run — which is why you watch it on a live dashboard rather than taking a promise.
