How We Staff the Floor

Recruit, train, test,
manage, replace

Staffing is not a one-time event here. It is a loop we run so your floor keeps performing.

  1. Recruit

    We source callers from a floor built specifically for outbound appointment setting in solar, roofing, and home services. You are not waiting on a fresh job posting. The pipeline already exists.

  2. Train

    Agents are trained on your campaign before they dial: your offer, your qualification criteria, and scripts refined across every campaign we have run. They arrive ready to hold a real conversation with a homeowner.

  3. Test

    Agents are tested on live calls for your specific campaign, not assumed to be a fit. The ones who can handle objections and book qualified appointments stay on your team.

  4. Manage

    Your floor is coached daily. Calls are listened to, agents are corrected, and the campaign is monitored every day rather than reviewed in a monthly summary. You watch the same numbers on a live dashboard.

  5. Replace

    If an agent still does not perform after coaching, they are replaced. That is our job, not yours, and because agents are dedicated to you the swap does not disrupt the rest of your team or your territory.

This is the same model behind our full builds. If you want the whole system rather than staffing alone, see build a call center and call center setup services. Straight Answers

Staffing questions
we get on every call

What do call center staffing solutions from Premura include?

A staffed, managed floor rather than a stack of resumes. We recruit, train, test, and manage dedicated agents who are exclusive to your territory, dial your script, and book appointments into your calendar. Daily coaching, a live dashboard, the dialer, and weekly-refreshed data are part of the same package.

Do I hire and manage the agents myself?

No, and that is the point of the model. We own recruiting, training, testing, daily coaching, and replacement. You approve the script and qualification criteria, talk to your agents directly, and watch results on a live dashboard, without running payroll or a hiring pipeline yourself.

What happens when an agent underperforms?

They are coached first, and if they still do not perform they are replaced. Agents are trained, tested, and replaced until they perform, so a weak seat does not become your problem. Because agents are dedicated to you, replacing one does not disrupt the rest of your team or your territory.

How many agents should I staff?

Start with the number that matches your closing capacity. Each dedicated agent targets roughly one qualified appointment per day, dialing 7–8 hours a day, so you staff to the calendar your closers can actually work. You can start with one agent and add more as you scale. Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call.

Are the agents shared with other companies?

No. Your agents are dedicated to you and exclusive to your territory. They dial the zip codes you choose, use the script you approve, and book into your calendar only. You are not sharing a pooled floor with other businesses calling the same homeowners.
