The Whole Operation

Everything we manage
so you do not have to

Running an outbound floor is not one job, it is seven. As your management company, Premura owns all of them.

  • Recruiting. We source and screen appointment setters continuously, because cold calling has heavy washout and a good floor is always hiring.
  • Training. New agents are trained on solar, roofing, and home-services scripts before they ever touch your territory.
  • Quality assurance. Every agent is held to the script and qualification standard you approve, with QA on live calls.
  • Dialer. We configure and operate the dialer per campaign, so it is part of the operation, not a subscription you manage.
  • Data. Our in-house scraping software builds and refreshes your calling lists weekly, filtered to your zip codes.
  • Coaching. Managers coach the floor daily on live calls and cut what is not working fast.
  • Dashboard. Production, dials, and booked appointments show up on a live dashboard you can open any time.
The Daily Rhythm

What managing your floor
looks like every day

Call centers decay without daily management. Here is the operating rhythm Premura runs while your calendar fills.

  1. The floor logs on and dials

    Your dedicated setters start their day on your territory only. They dial 7 to 8 hours, working the current data our software refreshed, not a list that has been called for months.

    Agents are exclusive to you, not shared across a rotation of companies.

  2. Managers listen and coach live

    Floor managers monitor calls as they happen, correct on the spot, and reinforce the parts of the script that are converting. Coaching is continuous, not a monthly review.

    Underperformers are tested and replaced until the floor performs.

  3. Qualified appointments hit your calendar

    Setters book qualified appointments straight into your closers' calendar. Roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day is the production we manage toward.

    No spreadsheet hand-off that turns into a no-show.

  4. You watch it on the dashboard

    Dials, contacts, and appointments show up live. You do not wait for a report to know how the operation is performing, and you can talk to your agents directly.

    Transparency is the default, not a request you have to make.

  5. We tune the operation weekly

    Data is refreshed, the script is adjusted against what real homeowners are saying, and the team is sized up or down as your closing capacity changes.

    You scale by adding agents, not by re-entering the hiring market.

Straight Answers

Choosing a call center
management company

What does a call center management company actually do?

A call center management company owns the daily operation of the phones so you do not have to. Premura recruits and trains the agents, configures and runs the dialer, builds and refreshes the calling data, enforces QA against a script you approve, coaches the floor every day, and reports production on a live dashboard. You keep the appointments and the closers, we run the machine that fills the calendar.

Do I get my own agents, or are they shared across other companies?

Your agents are dedicated to you and exclusive to your territory. They are not a shared pool dialing for several companies into the same neighborhoods. You pick the zip codes, the agents call only your territory, and they book qualified appointments straight into your calendar.

How do you manage quality and performance day to day?

Setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day against a script and qualification standard you approve. We coach on live calls daily, run QA, and test and replace agents until they perform. You watch it all on a live dashboard rather than waiting for a monthly summary, and you can talk to your agents directly.

Where does the calling data come from?

We build it in house. Our own scraping software generates and refreshes your calling lists weekly, filtered to the territory you choose. That keeps the floor working current data instead of a stale purchased list that other companies are calling at the same time.

What do I still control if you manage the operation?

You control the important things: the territory and zip codes worked, the script and qualification standard you approve, and how many agents you run. You also see production live on the dashboard. Premura handles recruiting, training, the dialer, data, QA, and daily coaching.

Is there a long-term contract, and how is it priced?

No long-term lock-in is required to start. Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a strategy call, because it depends on how many agents you run. You scale the operation by adding or removing agents rather than by hiring and firing in house.
