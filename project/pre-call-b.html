Commonly Asked Questions

01What does it cost, and can it scale?

Most clients land at $50 to $100 per qualified appointment once ramped. We don't charge per appointment, that number is the whole system cost divided by the appointments it books, and it scales with your closers.

02Does it work without door-to-door or ads?

Yes. Your own calling team books straight to your calendar. No knocking, no ads.

03Am I a fit if I do under 5 installs a month?

Probably not yet. You need room for 40 to 50 appointments and a closer ready to work them. If that's you, let's talk.

04Who needs to be on the call?

Everyone who runs or pays for the call center.

05How fast are we live?

Usually 1 to 3 weeks.

06Where can you run this?

Anywhere in the US. You pick the zip codes.

07Who hires, trains, and manages the agents?

We do all of it. You watch it live in your dashboard.

08What counts as a qualified appointment?

You set the rules. We only book appointments that meet them.

09What results are clients getting?

About one good appointment per agent per day.

10Can I see the scripts before launch?

Yes. You approve everything before we go live.

11What data do you call?

Our own data software, refreshed every week.

12Can I start small and grow?

Yes. Setters call all day, and you can add agents whenever you want.

13How do you avoid double-booking?

Our software books each closer's calendar with no overlap.

14Can I pause or add agents any time?

Yes. Add agents, cut agents, or pause any time.

15Do territories burn out?

No. We rotate zip codes so they stay fresh, and we can chase a storm fast.

16What if results dip?
