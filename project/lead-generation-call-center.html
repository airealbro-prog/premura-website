How It Works

From your zip codes
to a full calendar

You bring the market and the qualification bar. We bring the agents, the data, the tooling, and the daily management that turns dials into booked appointments.

  • You choose the territory. Pick the zip codes you want generated. Coverage is nationwide across the US, and your agents work your area only.
  • You define qualified. Homeowner status, bill or roof size, decision-maker, timeframe. We build it into the script and QA before calls begin.
  • We staff and coach the team. Dedicated agents are trained on your offer and coached daily. Hiring and turnover never touch your desk.
  • Setters dial fresh data daily. Seven to eight hours a day against in-house data refreshed weekly, screening to your bar on every call.
  • Appointments hit your calendar. Qualified meetings book directly onto your closers' calendars, all visible on a live dashboard. See the full how it works walkthrough.

Because the agents are dedicated and the data is your own, this is genuine lead generation, not lead reselling. It is also a proven alternative to knocking every door yourself. Compare on the door knocking alternative.

Straight Answers

Lead generation call center,
questions we get first

What does a lead generation call center do?

A lead generation call center staffs agents who dial out to prospects and turn conversations into qualified opportunities. Premura's version focuses on booked appointments: dedicated agents call your own territory using in-house data and set qualified appointments directly onto your calendar, so you generate demand rather than buy recycled leads.

Are these shared leads or my own?

Your own. Premura does not resell shared leads. Dedicated agents work only your zip codes and generate appointments that belong to your pipeline alone. The data is sourced in-house with scraping software refreshed weekly, not bought from a list broker selling the same names to competitors.

What counts as a qualified appointment?

You define the qualification bar, and agents screen to it on every call before booking. That typically includes homeowner status, bill or roof size, decision-maker present, and timeframe. The criteria are built into the script and QA during setup so what lands on your calendar is worth your closers' time.

How many leads or appointments will I get?

We manage to roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. Setters dial seven to eight hours a day, and you increase volume by adding dedicated agents rather than re-hiring in-house. Actual numbers vary by offer, territory, and season.

How is this priced?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, because it depends on how many agents you run and the campaign length. There is no long-term lock-in required to start, and you scale up or down by adding or removing agents.
