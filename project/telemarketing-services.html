  • Home improvement and remodeling. Windows, bath, HVAC, and general remodeling crews that need consultations booked instead of a rep dialing between jobs.

How It Works

From your zip codes
to appointments on your calendar

You are not buying a call list or a piece of software. You are buying a trained, managed calling operation that runs on your behalf.

  1. You pick the territory

    Choose the zip codes you want worked. Our in-house data and scraping software builds the calling lists and refreshes them weekly, so agents are always dialing live contacts in the areas you cover.

    Coverage is nationwide across the United States, and you decide exactly where the calls go.

  2. You approve the script and QA

    We bring scripts refined across the solar, roofing, and home-services campaigns we run, and adapt them to your offer. You sign off on the script and the quality standard before the first call, so the outreach sounds like your company.

    Nothing is said to a homeowner that you have not approved in advance.

  3. Trained agents call all day

    Dedicated setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day against your list. They are recruited, trained, and coached daily by Premura, and they qualify each prospect before anything reaches your calendar.

    The appointments you receive are pre-qualified against the criteria you set.

  4. Appointments book onto your calendar

    Qualified appointments drop directly into your calendar, and a live dashboard shows the dials, the sets, and the shows in real time. You spend your day running the appointments instead of chasing them.

    You watch the whole operation on the dashboard whenever you want to look.

Compliance First

Professional outreach,
DNC and TCPA handled

Cheap, careless telemarketing is a legal liability, not a bargain. Premura runs outbound the way it should be run.

  • TCPA and Do-Not-Call rules are built in. Outbound calling in the United States runs under these rules, and compliance is part of how the floor operates rather than something bolted on after a problem.
  • Your QA standard governs every call. You approve the quality standard, and agents are held to it through daily coaching and monitoring.
  • Dedicated agents, not a random overseas pool. The people calling for you are trained, managed, and accountable, working your account rather than churning through a dozen at once.
  • Real coaching, every day. Calls are listened to and setters are coached daily, so the outreach stays professional and on message over time.

Straight Answers

Telemarketing services,
what owners ask first

What telemarketing services does Premura provide?

Premura provides outbound telemarketing focused on booking appointments. Trained agents call your chosen territory using the script and qualification criteria you approve, and they set qualified appointments directly onto your calendar. It is built for home improvement, solar, and small business companies that want their phones ringing with real prospects rather than a raw list to chase.

Is your telemarketing compliant with DNC and TCPA rules?

Yes. Outbound calling in the United States runs under TCPA and Do-Not-Call rules, and compliance is built into how our floor operates rather than bolted on afterward. You approve the script and the QA standard, and calls follow that standard every time, so the outreach stays professional and above board.

Do you write the scripts or do I?

You approve every script and QA standard before a single call goes out. We bring scripts refined across the solar, roofing, and home-services campaigns we have run, then adapt them to your offer. Nothing is said to a homeowner that you have not signed off on.

Is this a good fit for a small home services business?

Yes. You do not have to hire, train, or manage a phone room to get the benefit of one. You pick the zip codes you want called, we supply dedicated trained agents, and you can start with a single agent and add more as the appointments prove out. There is no long-term lock-in required to begin.

How do you find people to call?

Our in-house data and scraping software builds the calling lists for the territory you choose and refreshes them weekly. Agents dial 7 to 8 hours a day against live contacts rather than a stale list, which is a large part of why the outreach produces appointments.

What will telemarketing services cost me?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, because it depends on how many agents you run and the territory they cover. There are no long-term contracts required to start, so you can begin small and scale based on results.
