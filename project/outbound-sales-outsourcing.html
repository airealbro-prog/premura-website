What You Hand Over

The whole top of the funnel,
run as a service

Outsourced SDR services should replace the entire prospecting operation, not just rent you a few dialers. Here is what a dedicated Premura team owns for you.

  1. We build the team

    We recruit and screen setters, then keep training and replacing until the seats perform. You never touch a job board, run an interview, or absorb the washout that cold calling always produces.

    Your version: dedicated agents pulled from a floor of 160+, assigned to your account.

  2. We feed them your territory

    You pick the zip codes you want worked. Our in-house data and scraping software builds the calling lists and refreshes them weekly, so your agents are always dialing live contacts rather than a stale list from last quarter.

    Your version: exclusive coverage of the areas you choose, nobody else calling them.

  3. They dial your script all day

    Setters spend 7 to 8 hours a day on the phone working the script and qualification criteria you approved. Every call follows the QA standard you sign off on, so the appointments that reach your calendar are pre-qualified, not just warm bodies.

    Your version: roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day is the number we manage to.

  4. Appointments land on your closers' calendars

    A qualified prospect is booked directly into your calendar. Your closers open their schedule and find confirmed appointments waiting, instead of spending their morning prospecting for the afternoon.

    Your version: a live dashboard shows the dials, the sets, and the shows in real time.

  5. We coach them daily

    Outbound teams decay without daily management. Premura listens to calls, coaches the setters, and cuts what is not working every day, so the team stays sharp long after the first week of enthusiasm fades.

    Your version: managed by Premura, watched by you through the dashboard whenever you want.

The Honest Comparison

Hire your own SDRs
or outsource the whole function

Build an in-house SDR team

Right if prospecting will be a permanent core competency

  • You own recruiting, screening, and the turnover that cold calling always produces
  • Payroll, dialer, data, and CRM are separate line items you have to integrate
  • Someone senior has to write scripts, run QA, and coach the floor every day
  • Ramp is measured in months before a new setter books reliably
  • Full ownership of the asset, which genuinely matters at large permanent scale

Outsource to Premura

Right if you want appointments, not a hiring project

  • The floor, the training, and the tech stack already exist and are running
  • Your agents are dedicated to you: your territory, your script, your calendar
  • Priced per dedicated agent, with agent, data, and daily management included
  • You approve scripts and QA, then watch results on a live dashboard
  • Scale by adding or removing agents, with no long-term lock-in to start

The honest cut: if outbound prospecting is going to be a permanent, large-scale function you want to own outright, building in-house eventually earns its keep. If what you actually need is your closers back on the phone with qualified prospects this quarter, outsource the front end and keep your team closing.

Straight Answers

Outbound sales outsourcing,
what buyers ask first

What is outbound sales outsourcing?

It means handing your outbound sales development, the prospecting and appointment-setting front end of your pipeline, to an outside team that runs it for you. With Premura you get a dedicated SDR team that is recruited, trained, and managed on our floor. They cold-call your exclusive territory and book qualified appointments directly onto your closers' calendars, so your in-house people spend their time closing rather than dialing.

Are the SDRs dedicated to my company or shared across clients?

Dedicated. Each agent works your territory and only your territory, using the script and qualification criteria you approve. They are not a shared pool splitting time across several companies, so the appointments they set are exclusively yours.

How many appointments should I expect per SDR?

Our setters dial 7 to 8 hours a day, and we manage to roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day. You pick the zip codes you want worked, and our in-house data software feeds each agent fresh contact lists that are refreshed weekly.

How is this different from hiring my own SDRs?

Hiring in-house means you own recruiting, screening, payroll, turnover, scripting, dialer setup, and daily coaching. Outsourcing to Premura means those pieces already exist. You approve the script and QA, watch performance on a live dashboard, and scale by adding or removing dedicated agents instead of re-entering the hiring market.

What does outbound sales outsourcing cost?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, because the right team size depends on your territory and your closers' capacity. There is no long-term lock-in required to start, so you can begin with one agent and expand as the appointments prove out.
