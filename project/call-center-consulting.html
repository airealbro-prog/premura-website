How It Works

From diagnosis
to a floor that dials

Consulting that ends in a running appointment-setting team, not a PDF. Here is the path we walk with you.

  1. We audit your current outbound reality

    On a strategy call we map your territory, your closers' capacity, your existing data, and any calling you are doing now. The goal is a clear read on where booked appointments are leaking out of your funnel.

    You leave the call with a diagnosis you could act on with or without us.

  2. We design the team and the script

    We size the number of dedicated agents to your zip codes and closer capacity, then shape the script, qualification questions, and objection handling. You approve every word before it goes live.

    Scripts are refined from every solar and roofing campaign we have run.

  3. We stand up the data and dialer

    Our in-house scraping software builds and refreshes your calling lists weekly, filtered to your territory, and feeds the dialer. No stale purchased list that everyone else is already calling.

    Data discipline is the part most consultants never touch.

  4. We recruit, train, and put agents on the phones

    Premura hires and trains the setters, who dial 7 to 8 hours a day and book qualified appointments straight into your calendar. Roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day is the volume we manage toward.

    Agents are tested and replaced until they perform.

  5. We run it and coach it every day

    Daily coaching on live calls, QA against the standard you approved, and a live dashboard so you see production without waiting for a report. If something is not working, we cut it fast.

    A weekly check-in is not management. Daily is.

Straight Answers

Call center consulting,
the questions we hear first

What is call center consulting, and how is Premura different?

Traditional call center consulting hands you a report and a plan, then leaves you to hire, train, and manage the floor yourself. Premura consults the same way, diagnosing your outbound setup honestly, but we do not stop at advice. We build the appointment-setting team and run it day to day, so the plan turns into booked appointments instead of a document on a shelf.

Do you only advise, or do you actually run the call center?

Both, and that is the point. We advise on structure, scripts, data, and staffing, then we recruit, train, and manage dedicated agents who call your territory. You can take our diagnosis and run with it yourself, or have us operate the floor. Most clients choose the done-with-you-and-run-for-you path because running a floor daily is a full-time job.

What do you look at when you diagnose my outbound setup?

We review who is calling and how many hours a day they actually dial, the quality and freshness of your calling data, your script and objection handling, how appointments reach your closers, and whether anyone coaches the floor daily. Most outbound problems trace back to thin data, a weak script, or no daily management, and we address all three.

How are the agents staffed and managed after the consult?

Agents are dedicated to you and exclusive to your territory, not shared across other companies. Premura recruits, trains, and manages them, with setters dialing 7 to 8 hours a day and daily coaching on live calls. You approve the scripts and QA standards, watch results on a live dashboard, and talk to your agents directly.

Is there a long-term contract to get started?

No long-term lock-in is required to start. Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a strategy call, since it depends on how many agents you run. You pick the zip codes you want worked, and you can scale the team by adding or removing agents.
