Which industries do you actually serve?

Core client base: solar and roofing — published on camera with real names and numbers. Campaigns also run for window and door companies. HVAC, pest control, remodeling and storm restoration are expansion verticals — same floors, training and management, adapted to a new industry, and we say so plainly instead of claiming experience we haven't published.

Does the same model really work across industries?

The machine is industry-agnostic: dedicated agents, refined script, configured dialer and CRM, live calendar sync, daily management. What changes is the script, the qualification bar and the seasonal calling rhythm — exactly what the 14-day setup configures for your vertical.

How is it priced?

Per dedicated agent — priced per agent per month depending on team size and length, everything included. 5+ qualified appointments per agent per week is the managed floor. No contracts to start the conversation.

Are appointments exclusive?

Structurally, yes: your agent calls only your territory and books only your calendar. There's no marketplace, so there's nothing to resell.
