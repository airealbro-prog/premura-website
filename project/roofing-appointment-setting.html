Why Roofers Outsource The Dials

Your crew climbs roofs
Someone else has to book them

  • Inspections are the whole funnel. A roofing sale starts with a homeowner agreeing to a look at the roof. That first yes is a phone job, and it is the one your estimators are worst positioned to chase while they are already on a ladder across town.
  • Storms move faster than a static list. After hail or high wind, the homeowners who need you are concentrated in a few zip codes for a short window. A dedicated agent can be pointed at that map the same week, booking inspections while the damage is fresh.
  • Exclusive beats shared, every time. A shared roofing lead is sold to several contractors, so you compete on speed-to-dial with people quoting the same roof. An appointment your own setter books belongs to you and nobody else.
  • Consistent pipeline between storms. Retail reroofs, aging shingles and insurance follow-ups keep a setter productive in the quiet stretches, so your calendar is not empty until the next weather event.
How A Roofing Campaign Runs

From storm map
to booked inspection

A roofing campaign is a managed calling operation pointed at the territory you choose. Here is the sequence.

  1. You choose the territory and the trigger

    Pick your zip codes, and tell us whether this is a steady retail campaign or a storm response. Coverage is US wide, and your setter dials only the area you name.

    In-house data software builds the list and refreshes it weekly, so a storm map stays current.

  2. We assign and train the setter

    Premura recruits, screens and trains the agent from a floor of 160 plus callers, then manages them daily. Roofing objections are their own craft, so agents are coached on the roof-inspection conversation specifically.

    QA and daily coaching keep the setter effective through the length of the campaign.

  3. You approve the script and the bar

    You sign off on the pitch and on what makes an inspection worth sending a crew to: homeowner status, roof age, storm damage, service area. The setter qualifies against your bar before booking anything.

    Scripts adapted for retail reroofs and storm restoration alike, tuned to your offer.

  4. The inspection lands on your calendar

    A qualified inspection is booked as a specific slot directly into your calendar, ready for your crew or estimator. No lead list to work later, just a confirmed time.

    A live dashboard shows dials, contacts and booked inspections whenever you check.

Straight Answers

Roofing appointment setting
questions we hear first

What does a roofing appointment setter actually book?

A confirmed roof inspection or consultation at a specific time, placed directly onto your calendar. The agent cold-calls homeowners in your territory, opens the roofing conversation, qualifies them against criteria you set such as homeowner status, roof age or storm damage, and locks in a time your crew can keep. You get a booked appointment, not a name to chase.

Are the roofing appointments exclusive to me?

Yes. Your setter is dedicated to your company and dials only your zip codes. Unlike a shared roofing lead that a broker sells to three or four contractors at once, an appointment your agent books is yours and is never resold. You are not racing another roofer to call the same homeowner first. More on this on exclusive roofing leads.

Can setters handle storm restoration campaigns?

Yes. Storm restoration is time sensitive, so after a hail or wind event a dedicated agent can work the affected zip codes quickly and book inspections while homeowners are actively looking. Because the in-house data software refreshes weekly and you choose the territory, campaigns can follow the storm map rather than a static list.

How fast can a roofing campaign start?

We recruit, train and manage agents from an existing floor of more than 160 trained callers, so a roofing campaign does not start from an empty office. You choose the zip codes, approve the roofing script and qualification bar, and the setter begins dialing your territory seven to eight hours a day with a live dashboard tracking every call.

What does roofing appointment setting cost?

Pricing is per dedicated agent and depends on how many setters you run and for how long, with no long-term lock-in to start. The exact figure is discussed on a strategy call after we map your territory and size the team, so the quote reflects your real campaign rather than a generic rate.
