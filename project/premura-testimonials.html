Every story is different, but the shape tends to rhyme. Here is the before-and-after pattern most of these operators describe when they explain what Premura changed.

Before Premura

Growth capped by the owner's own hours

  • Appointments depended on door knocking or the owner chasing leads
  • The calendar swung between feast and famine week to week
  • Scaling meant personally hiring, training, and babysitting callers
  • Time went to running an outbound operation instead of closing

After Premura

A dedicated floor doing the calling

  • Trained agents cold-call the client's territory every day
  • Qualified appointments land straight on the closer's calendar
  • Premura recruits, trains, and manages the agents day to day
  • The owner watches results on a live dashboard and sells

The questions people ask

What are these testimonials?

They are the stories behind the numbers. Instead of a wall of star ratings, each testimonial is a real solar operator describing what they were doing before Premura, what changed when we built and managed their dedicated call center, and the result they now report on camera. You can watch every one in full on our reviews page.

Are the testimonials scripted?

No. The videos are clients speaking in their own words about their own operations. We do not put words in their mouths or publish an aggregate rating we made up. The numbers each person states are their individual outcomes, not a promise of what you will do.

What was life like before Premura for most clients?

A common thread is that growth depended on the owner or a small team knocking doors and chasing leads, so the calendar was inconsistent and scaling meant personally hiring and managing callers. The change clients describe is handing the outbound calling to a dedicated, managed floor so appointments arrive steadily on the calendar.

Can I talk to a current client?

We are happy to talk through references on a strategy call. In the meantime, the video testimonials on our reviews page are named operators describing their own results, and Premura Corp is a Florida corporation founded in September 2022 with a public Better Business Bureau profile you can verify independently.

Will I get the same results as these clients?

Maybe more, maybe less. The results in these stories, from 17 deals in a first 30 days to 50 to 60 appointments a month, are individual outcomes that depend on the client's offer, territory, and close rate. We manage toward roughly one qualified appointment per agent per day and are honest that the rest depends on your sales process.
