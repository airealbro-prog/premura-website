Is Premura Right For You?

Who is Premura for?
An honest fit guide

Premura is a paid, done-for-you service, so the right question is not whether it works, it is whether it fits your company right now. Here is a candid look at who gets real value from dedicated agents and who should wait. We would rather you self-select out than sign up before you are ready.

The Honest Answer

A great fit for some companies,
the wrong move for others

Dedicated agents dial your territory all day and book appointments straight into your calendar. That is powerful if you can close and service them, and a waste if you cannot. Read both columns before you decide.

Premura is a strong fit

You already sell and can absorb volume

  • You run a solar, roofing, or home-services company
  • You have at least one closer, or a real closing process, ready to work the appointments
  • You are doing roughly five or more installs or jobs a month already
  • You could handle 40 to 50 new appointments a month without dropping the ones you have
  • You want a steady, full calendar rather than an occasional trickle of leads

Premura is not the fit yet

Better to wait and build first

  • You are very early and still figuring out your offer or pricing
  • You have no one to close, so appointments would sit unworked
  • You are doing under about five installs a month and cannot yet service more
  • You want us to sell and close for you, not just book qualified appointments
  • You need a handful of appointments a month, which is less than a dedicated agent produces

If you landed in the right column, that is not a rejection. It is a sequence. Build the closing side first, then a dedicated agent becomes an accelerator instead of a cost.

The Readiness Check

Four things that tell you
you are ready for dedicated agents

Every dedicated agent dials seven to eight hours a day and produces around one qualified appointment per agent per day. To turn that into revenue, four things need to already be true.

  • You are in the right industry. Our agents, scripts, and QA are built for solar, roofing, and home services. That focus is why they perform, and it is why we are honest that other industries are outside what we do.
  • You have closers with capacity. We book appointments into your calendar, and your team runs them. If your closers are already maxed out, more appointments create no-shows, not sales.
  • You have proven you can install. If you are already completing about five or more jobs a month, you have a working delivery engine that more appointments can feed. If you are not there yet, more volume is not your bottleneck.
  • You can absorb 40 to 50 appointments a month. One agent produces a full calendar. Before you add volume, make sure the appointments you already generate are not slipping through the cracks.
Why We Tell People No

Being candid about fit
is part of the service

A lot of appointment and lead companies will take anyone with a credit card. We do not, because it does not work for anyone when we do. If your capacity cannot keep up with a dedicated agent, the appointments go stale, the numbers look bad, and everyone is frustrated.

So on a strategy call we will ask direct questions about your closers, your current install volume, and your calendar. If the honest answer is that you are too early, we will say so and tell you what to build first. That is better for you, and it is better for us. Our best clients are companies that were ready, plugged agents into an operation that could handle the flow, and saw the results our reviews describe.

Straight Answers

Is Premura right for me?
The questions people ask

Who is Premura the best fit for?

Premura fits solar, roofing, and home-services companies that already close deals and have the capacity to work a full calendar of fresh appointments. If you have at least one closer, install or complete roughly five or more jobs a month, and could handle 40 to 50 new appointments a month, dedicated agents give you a reliable way to keep that pipeline full.

Who should not buy Premura?

If you are too early, have no one to close the appointments, or are doing under about five installs a month, we will usually tell you to wait. Booked appointments only turn into revenue if someone can run them and close them. Buying appointment capacity before you can service it wastes both your money and the agents' work.

Do I need my own closers to work with Premura?

Yes. Premura books qualified appointments straight into your calendar, but you or your team close them. We are the front of your funnel, not your sales team. If you do not yet have a closer or a reliable closing process, that is the piece to build first.

What if I only need a few appointments a month?

A dedicated agent dials seven to eight hours a day and produces around one qualified appointment per agent per day, so the model is built for companies that want a full, steady calendar. If your capacity is only a handful of appointments a month, a dedicated agent is more volume than you can use right now.

How do I know if I am ready before booking a call?

Ask yourself three things. Do you sell solar, roofing, or a home service. Can someone close the appointments we send. Could you handle 40 to 50 new appointments a month without dropping the ones you already have. If you answer yes to all three, book a call. If not, it is honest to wait until you can.
