How It Works

You set the bar,
we fill the calendar

The service runs on criteria you own. You decide what a qualified appointment is, and dedicated agents screen every prospect to that standard before anything reaches your closers.

  • You define qualified. Homeowner status, bill or roof size, decision-maker present, timeframe. We build your criteria into the script and QA before calls start.
  • Dedicated agents get trained. Setters assigned to you learn your offer and objections, and they call your territory only. Recruiting and turnover stay on our side.
  • They dial your data daily. Seven to eight hours a day against in-house data refreshed weekly, screening to your qualification bar on every call.
  • Appointments land on your calendars. Qualified meetings book directly onto your closers' calendars, tracked and visible on a live dashboard.
  • You scale by adding agents. One setter or a team of five, with no long-term lock-in required to start. See the full how it works walkthrough.

Because the agents are dedicated and exclusive to your territory, the appointments they set belong to your pipeline alone. Prefer to pay per appointment instead of per agent? Compare the models on pay per appointment vs dedicated.

Straight Answers

B2B appointment setting,
questions we get first

What is a B2B appointment setting service?

A B2B appointment setting service supplies trained agents who reach prospects in your market and book qualified meetings so your closers can spend their time selling instead of prospecting. Premura provides dedicated setters, the dialer and data, and your approved qualification criteria, then books appointments straight onto your closers' calendars.

Who sets the qualification criteria for an appointment?

You do. You define what makes an appointment qualified, for example homeowner status, roof or bill size, decision-maker present, and timeframe, and your agents screen to that bar on every call. We build the criteria into the script and QA during setup so your closers only see appointments worth their time.

What industries do you set appointments for?

Premura specializes in solar, roofing, and home-services companies. Agents are trained on the offer and objections specific to your vertical, and the qualification bar is built for how homeowners actually buy in that industry.

How do appointments reach my team?

Every qualified appointment is booked directly onto your closers' calendars, not handed off in a spreadsheet. You watch dials, contacts, and booked meetings on a live dashboard, and your dedicated agents work only your territory and zip codes.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No long-term lock-in is required to start. Pricing is per dedicated agent and is discussed on a call, since it depends on how many agents you run and the length of the campaign. You scale up or down by adding or removing dedicated agents.
