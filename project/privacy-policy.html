Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Premura Corp ("Premura", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit premura.org, contact us, or opt in to receive communications from us. By using our website or providing your information, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: name, business name, email address, phone number, and any details you submit through our forms or when you contact us.
  • Automatically collected: IP address, browser type, device information, and site-usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To schedule, confirm, and follow up on appointments and meetings.
  • To process and communicate about job applications and interviews.
  • To send service, account, and transactional messages (including by SMS, where you have opted in).
  • To improve our website and operations, and to comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

Consent. By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you consent to receive recurring automated text messages from Premura Corp at the number provided. Message types include appointment confirmations and reminders, disposition and account follow-ups, and (for applicants) job-application and interview updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or email support@premura.org.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

We do not share or sell your mobile phone number or your SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Information obtained as part of the SMS program is used only to operate that program.

How to opt in: you may opt in through our SMS opt-in form, by checking the SMS-consent box on an application or intake form, or as described in a service agreement with Premura.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (for example, CRM, telephony, and messaging vendors), when required by law, or to protect our rights. We do not sell your personal information. As stated above, mobile opt-in data and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for marketing.

5. Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies and analytics tools to understand site usage and improve performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal requirements, and we use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Choices & Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing communications at any time. To make a request, email support@premura.org.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.
