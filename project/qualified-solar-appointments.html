The Definition

Four things are true
before it hits your calendar

  • A real conversation happened. A trained agent spoke with a human being. Not a web form, not a clicked ad, not an "interested" checkbox from six months ago.
  • It's the homeowner. The person who can actually say yes to a roof — confirmed in conversation, not assumed from a data broker's file.
  • Roof and interest are confirmed against your criteria. Our agents qualify roof condition and genuine interest live on the call, to the standard you define on day one — your definition of qualified, not ours.
  • It's on your closer's calendar. Booked into a real time slot with live calendar sync the moment the call ends. An appointment in a spreadsheet is a no-show with extra steps.

Miss any of the four and it isn't a qualified appointment — it's a lead wearing the word as a costume. Most of what's sold as "qualified solar appointments" fails the first check alone.

Straight Answers

Qualified-appointment questions
we get on every call

What exactly makes an appointment "qualified"?

A trained agent had a live conversation with a real human, confirmed they're the homeowner, checked roof condition and genuine interest against your criteria, and booked a specific time slot onto your closer's calendar. Miss any step and it's a lead, not a qualified appointment.

How many can one agent produce?

5+ qualified appointments per agent per week is the floor we manage to — roughly 20+ a month per dedicated agent. Volume scales by adding agents.

Are they exclusive to my company?

Yes — structurally. Your agent dials only your territory and books only your calendar. There's no marketplace involved, so there's nothing to resell. Purchased solar leads, by contrast, are commonly sold to several installers at once.

How fast do appointments start?

Campaigns go live within 14 days of signup — agents trained on your offer, dialer loaded with your territory, CRM synced to your calendars. Appointments start landing once the campaign is live.
